About the Acharya

Acharya Dr. Rajnath Jha

Ph.D. in Astrology | Director — Jyotirveda Vigyan Sansthan | 30+ Years of Vedic Wisdom

🕉️ Renowned Astrologer, Patna

A Life Dedicated to Vedic Sciences

Acharya Dr. Rajnath Jha (Ph.D. in Astrology) is one of the most renowned astrologers of Bihar and serves as the Director of Jyotirveda Vigyan Sansthan. Honored with numerous national and international awards, he is an expert in Vedic astrology, horoscope analysis, palmistry, Vastu Shastra and numerology, and has guided millions of people toward happiness and success.

For 17 years, he served as the Jyotish & Karmkand consultant at Jyotish Mandap, Mahavir Mandir Trust, Patna. He gained widespread recognition through the "Kaal Dhamal" program on Radio Mirchi 98.3 FM, and has been associated with leading newspapers (Hindustan, Dainik Jagran, Dainik Bhaskar) and television channels (Maurya TV, ETV Bihar-Jharkhand, News18, Kashish News, Darsh TV and others).

He has delivered lectures at Banaras Hindu University (BHU) and KSDSU Darbhanga, and presented research papers on earthquakes, cancer, arthritis and asthma through the lens of astrology. His accurate predictions include India's 2011 Cricket World Cup victory, earthquakes in Bihar & Jharkhand, and major political developments.

Currently, Dr. Jha is laying the foundation of a Gurukul in Madhubani, Bihar, with the mission to preserve and revive the ancient Vedic and Vedanga education system.
