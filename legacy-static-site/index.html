About the Acharya

Acharya Dr. Rajnath Jha

Acharya Dr. Rajnath Jha (Ph.D. in Astrology) is one of the most renowned astrologers and serves as the Director of Jyotirveda Vigyan Sansthan. He has been honored with numerous national and international awards for his outstanding contributions to the field. Dr. Jha is an expert in astrology, horoscope analysis, palmistry, Vastu Shastra, and numerology, and has guided millions of people toward happiness and success through his insightful consultations.

For 30+ years, he served as an astrology and ritual consultant at Jyotish Mandap under the Mahavir Mandir Trust in Patna. He gained widespread recognition through the “Kaal Dhamal” program on Radio Mirchi 98.3 FM. In addition, he has been associated with leading newspapers (Hindustan, Dainik Jagran, Dainik Bhaskar) and television channels (Maurya TV Bihar Jharkhand, ETV Bihar Jharkhand, News18 Bihar Jharkhand, Kashish News Bihar Jharkhand, Darsh TV, and others), offering spiritual articles, blogs, and guidance on Indian festivals and celestial insights.

Dr. Jha has delivered lectures at prominent universities such as Banaras Hindu University (BHU) and KSDSU, and has presented research papers on various topics in astrology including earthquakes, cancer, arthritis, and asthma. His accurate predictions have covered political shifts, natural disasters, cricket outcomes, and agricultural trends. Some of his well-known predictions include:

  • India’s victory in the 2011 Cricket World Cup
  • Earthquakes in Bihar and Jharkhand
  • Predictions regarding Lalu Yadav’s health and political career

Currently, Dr. Jha is laying the foundation of a Gurukul in Madhubani, Bihar, with the mission to preserve and revive the ancient Vedic and Vedanga education system.
